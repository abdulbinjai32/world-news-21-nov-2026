One page of Saturday 21 November 2026's world news: 40 stories across seven regions,
every one of them dated, drawn as a front page and rendered deterministically to a
1024x1024 PNG. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Sum up 21 November's world news in one image".

The page leads on COP31 running into a 13th day in Antalya, three Houthi ballistic
missiles downed over Riyadh, and Russia's overnight barrage on Ukraine; then carries
Iran and Hormuz, Gaza, the Peru earthquake, the Geneva talks on autonomous weapons,
bitcoin's slide, and the day's disasters, diplomacy and sport, with a map of where the
numbered stories happened and a date-check note.

Every story happened, or was first reported, on 21 November 2026. Counts and claims are
attributed to the bodies that made them (the Saudi-led coalition, the IRGC, USGS, the
UN, wire services). Friday's COP31 draft and the Russian missiles that came down in
Poland are shown as Saturday's deadlock and fallout.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.png from entry.png
```

`tools/render.py` is the program that drew the page (Python 3 + Pillow, fixed inputs,
no randomness).
